source. It is a **leaf transitive** of `httpcore` (via `httpx`) and should stay that way.

## 3. Role in the app's stack

**Direct use: none.** Repo-wide grep of `*.py` outside `.venv` for `import h11` / `from h11` /
`h11.` → **zero hits**; `user_scanner` likewise (`grep -rn "import h11" user_scanner/` → none).

**Indirect use: total.** The app's HTTP client is httpx — `src\services\http_client.py:17`
`import httpx`, `:38` `def get_client() -> httpx.AsyncClient`, `:49` `httpx.Limits(...)`, `:54`
`httpx.Timeout(...)`, `:58` `httpx.AsyncHTTPTransport(retries=retries, limits=limits, http2=True)`.
Also in `src\main.py`, `src\components\result_card.py`, `src\core\logger_handler.py`,
`src\services\cache_service.py`.

The scanner engines are the volume driver: `user_scanner` holds **2,741 site modules**
(`email_scan` 240 + `user_scan` 2,536, excl. `__init__.py`), and **every one references httpx
(0 without)**; 201 import it directly. Every such call — and each of the app's own httpx calls —
frames and parses HTTP/1.1 through h11 via httpcore's `HTTP11Connection`
(`user_scanner\core\helpers.py:17` `import httpx`; `:579` `httpx.AsyncClient(proxy=proxy, timeout=timeout)`).

**Capability provided:** the *correctness* layer for HTTP/1.1 on the wire — chunked
transfer-encoding, `Content-Length` vs. EOF body framing, keep-alive reuse rules, header
parsing/normalisation, 1xx informational responses, and the request/response state machine that
forbids illegal sequences. Pure protocol library: no sockets, no threads, no async, no I/O — it
cannot block, leak handles, or couple to the event loop.

## 4. Verdict

**N/A for direct use. PERFECT as transitive.** App and engines **do not use h11 directly**
(exhaustive grep of app source and `user_scanner`) — nothing to migrate, refactor, or vendor. As
the leaf under `httpx → httpcore → h11`, it delivers **HTTP/1.1 wire correctness behind every
user-scanner httpx call** — 2,741 site modules plus the app's own pooled client
(`src\services\http_client.py`): load-bearing for real traffic, invisible to our code.

**No action required.** Do not add a direct dependency, do not pin separately from the lockfile,
do not vendor. Keep it where `uv.lock:191` puts it: 0.16.0, `py3-none-any`, zero-dep.

**Residual risk: minimal.** Pure-Python, no native code, no I/O, no runtime side effects, MIT,
actively maintained (0.16.0 released 2025-04-24 per the `uv.lock` sdist `upload-time`).

**Nuance:** `http2=True` (`src\services\http_client.py:58`) routes negotiated HTTP/2 to
`h2`/`hpack`/`hyperframe`, so h11 governs the **HTTP/1.1** requests only. Both paths correct.
